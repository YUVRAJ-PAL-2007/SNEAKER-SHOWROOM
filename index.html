How a pair gets on a plinth

Three checks, every time. If a pair fails one of them, it goes back to the seller.

  • We buy it outright

    Nothing here is on consignment, so we have no reason to talk up a pair we are not sure about. If it is on the floor, our own money is already in it.

  • Two authenticators, separately

    Stitch count, sole date stamp, box label, glue lines. Two people check the same pair without seeing each other's notes, and both have to agree.

  • The file comes with it

    You leave with the photographs, the seller history and the condition report. Resell it in ten years and that file is worth real money to you.

One hour, one visitor at a time.

The room holds twelve plinths and one person. Book an hour, tell us which pairs you want out of the case, and we will have them unboxed and under the light when you arrive.

Trying on is expected. We keep liners and a bench by the door.
